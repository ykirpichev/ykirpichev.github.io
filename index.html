Study · RL optimization · TPU v6e

Async vs sync RL rollouts on TPU v6e

A controlled GRPO comparison on Qwen3.5-2B / AIME. Overlapping generation and learning with bounded staleness cuts wall-clock time without a measurable quality gap.

1.61×faster to 40 steps (53 vs 85 min)
1.7%rollout idle, down from 39.5%
≈ equalpass@1: 8.96% async vs 9.38% sync
≈ $22total compute cost
