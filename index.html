Multi-teacher on-policy distillation on TPU v6e

Merging a math specialist and an instruction-following specialist into one 2B student, compared with joint RL, offline distillation and weight merging.

+6 pp from routing on instruction following; offline KD and a task-arithmetic merge match MOPD
Nov 2026 · Qwen3.5-2B · 4 seeds × 60 steps · ≈ $596
Same-origin teachers close to the student; the lead over joint RL holds only on IFBench.

 RL optimization · TPU v6e

Async vs sync RL rollouts on TPU v6e

GRPO on AIME with generation and training overlapped at one step of policy lag, with and without an importance-sampling correction.

1.72× faster per training step than sync, with no loss of accuracy (20.2% vs 18.5% pass@1)
Nov 2026 · Qwen3.5-2B · 60 steps · ≈ $204 across all rounds
The accuracy difference is not significant. Without the correction, async stalls after step 40.
